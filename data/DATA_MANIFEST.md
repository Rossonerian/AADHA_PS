# Data Manifest

Every demo and seed record MUST carry `data_origin: "real" | "synthetic"` per [`docs/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` Section 2](docs/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md#2-rules-that-override-everything).

> Production seeding policy: `infra/scripts/seed.py` loads **content tables only** (`questions`, `lessons`, `scenarios`, `edu_explain_cache`) and validates market datasets. User and persona study fixtures (`study_seed.yaml`, `data/seed/verifier/seed.py`) are restricted to offline tests/evaluation and are never inserted into live Neon Postgres.
